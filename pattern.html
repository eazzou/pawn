Approach

Clarity first

The challenge

Reduce the friction of a first visit.

First-time customers may not know how a pawn transaction differs from an outright sale. The design separates the three journeys and explains the next step without pressure.

01 / Understand

Identify likely questions: what can I bring, what happens to my item, and what choices do I have?

02 / Organize

Group the information by customer intent rather than store inventory.

03 / Design

Use clear calls to action, a reusable card system and responsive layouts.

The solution

Three paths. One clear starting point.

Pawn

Use an item as collateral for a loan. Review terms and redemption details.

Learn about pawning

Sell

Receive an offer for an item you no longer need.

Trade

Explore secondhand goods and trade-in options.

Next steps

Designed to earn trust.

Validate comprehension with usability testing, assess keyboard navigation and refine category labels. This is a concept, not a live pawn service.
